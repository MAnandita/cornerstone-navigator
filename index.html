How it works

Built on a real architecture,
not just an API call.

01

Ingest

Connect your Gmail. Navigator pulls in ParentSquare emails and stores them as structured posts with per-user visibility metadata.

02

Embed

Each post is turned into a 768-dimensional vector that captures its meaning, plus structured entities — events, deadlines, people, programs.

03

Retrieve

When you ask a question, four ranking signals — semantic similarity, keyword match, recency, and tags — surface the most relevant posts.

04

Answer

A language model reads the retrieved posts and writes a cited answer. Every claim links to the source. Nothing is invented.
