How it works

What happens when you
ask a question.

01

Ingest

Connect your Gmail. Navigator pulls in ParentSquare emails and stores them as structured posts with per-user visibility metadata.

02

Embed

Each post is turned into a 768-dimensional vector that captures its meaning, plus structured entities including events, deadlines, people, and programs.

03

Retrieve

When you ask a question, four ranking signals — semantic similarity, keyword match, recency, and tags — surface the most relevant posts.

04

Answer

A language model reads the retrieved posts and writes a cited answer. Every claim links to its source. Nothing is invented.
