The problem with how schools communicate.

Why I built Navigator AI, and why it matters.

Driving question

Can a school-specific AI assistant make families' lives meaningfully easier by turning unstructured school communications into clear, on-demand answers?

The problem

[WRITE 300–400 WORDS TOTAL ACROSS THIS PAGE]

Modern schools communicate through an ever-growing number of channels: ParentSquare announcements, weekly newsletters, Canvas updates, teacher emails, athletics reminders. Each message is individually short, but in aggregate they create a firehose that most working parents cannot keep up with.

The tools we have to manage this — email, ParentSquare, Canvas — were not designed to synthesize across sources. They store information; they do not surface it. A parent who wants to know "what's happening this week" has to scroll through dozens of messages and assemble the answer themselves.
