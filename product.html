What Navigator actually does.

A working private chatbot for school communities — deployed and piloted at KLS.

The primary surface. Users ask questions in plain English and receive answers grounded in their own school posts, with citations linked back to the original messages.

A structured view of what's actually upcoming over the next four weeks. Sourced from entity records extracted at ingestion time, filtered for freshness, deduplicated.

Posts archive

Every ingested message, searchable and filterable. Strict per-user scoping means parents and staff at the same school never see each other's inbox content.
